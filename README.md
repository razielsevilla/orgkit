# OrgKit

**One simple workspace to run a student organization, and to hand it over cleanly when the officers change.**

## The problem

Student organizations run on goodwill and group chats. Member lists live in a
spreadsheet nobody updates. Dues are tracked in someone's notes app. Attendance
is a sign-up sheet that gets lost. And every year, when officers graduate or
step down, most of what they knew leaves with them: where the files are, who
paid, what was promised to whom.

The new officers start from scratch. Members get chased for dues twice, or not at
all. Good events are forgotten because nobody wrote down how they were run.

## What OrgKit does

OrgKit puts the everyday work of an org in one place, built for officers who
have classes to attend and no time to learn complicated software.

- **Members.** Share a link or QR code. Students join in a minute and agree to
  how their information is used, so you have a clean, consented roster.
- **Officers and roles.** Invite your team and decide who can do what, such as
  admin, finance or general officer.
- **Events and attendance.** Members scan a QR code to check in. You see who is
  there live, and every member has an attendance history.
- **Dues.** Set a collection period, see how much is collected and who is still
  missing, review payments, and send reminders to those who haven't paid.
- **Terms.** Keep track of each academic year and who held which role.
- **Documents.** Keep the constitution, permits, signatory forms and key links in
  one place instead of in someone's personal drive.
- **Handover.** Outgoing officers fill in a guide for their successors: what they
  do, what is ongoing, who to contact, what is still open. The past events log
  and documents are already there waiting.
- **Org health at a glance.** The home screen shows how the org is doing and
  what to do next, in plain words: dues falling behind, members waiting to be
  confirmed, no upcoming events.

It works on a phone, because that is where most students will use it.

## Who it is for

- **Student officers** who want to spend their time on the org, not on admin.
- **Incoming officers** who want to inherit a working org instead of a mystery.
- **Members** who just want to join, check in and pay dues without friction.
- **Faculty advisers and school offices** who want orgs that stay organized from
  one year to the next.

## Privacy and trust

Officers sign in with Google. Members only share what they agree to, and they
are told how it is used when they join. Each org can only see its own data.

## Status

OrgKit is under active development. Accounts, member joining, officer roles,
events with QR check-in, dues, documents, handover and the org health view are
in place. Work continues on making it dependable for real organizations.
